1. Access and roles

FlashPOS uses individual accounts so every important action can be attributed to the person who performed it.

Sign in

1. Open the production web address in a current browser. For local use, open http://localhost:3000.

2. Enter the email address and password issued by the super administrator.

3. Select Sign in to FlashPOS. After authentication, the Dashboard opens.

4. Confirm that your name and role appear at the lower-left of the navigation panel.

No public registration
Users cannot create their own accounts from the login page. A super administrator creates each employee account.

Role permissions

CAPABILITYEMPLOYEESUPER ADMIN
Create sales and print receiptsYesYes
Add/edit products and adjust stockYesYes
Archive or permanently delete productsNoYes
View purchase orders and receive stockYesYes
Create suppliers and purchase ordersNoYes
Issue item refunds and restock returnsNoYes
View full profit and expense informationLimitedYes
Manage users, settings, tools, and activity logNoYes

Account safety

• Use a separate account for every employee; do not share one cashier login.

• Never send passwords or Supabase secret keys through chat or public messages.

• Select Sign out when leaving the terminal, especially on a shared computer.

• Ask a super administrator to deactivate an account immediately when access is no longer required.

3. Point of Sale

Use Point of Sale for every walk-in, TikTok, Lazada, and Shopee order so stock, reports, and closing totals remain accurate.

Create a sale

1. Open Point of Sale from the navigation panel.

2. Choose the order source: Walk-in, TikTok, Lazada, or Shopee.

3. Search by product name, SKU, or barcode, then select the required piece or box option.

4. Enter the quantity. Review the cart and confirm that enough stock is available.

5. For Walk-in, choose Cash or GCash. For GCash, enter the transaction reference ID.

6. For TikTok, Lazada, or Shopee, enter the marketplace order ID shown on the platform.

7. Review subtotal, the automatic 3% non-VAT percentage charge, and the final total.

8. Complete the sale, then print or save the receipt if needed.

Source and payment requirements

Duplicate references are blocked
If a GCash reference or marketplace order ID has already been used, verify the order before trying again. Do not invent a replacement reference.

Completing a sale deducts the equivalent number of pieces. One box deducts the product’s configured pieces-per-box quantity.

4. Transactions and receipts

Transactions is the permanent operational history of sales, fulfillment updates, receipts, and authorized refunds.

Find and review a transaction

1. Open Transactions and locate the sale by receipt number, date, channel, or customer/order reference.

2. Open the transaction to review items, quantities, source, payment, percentage charge, and total.

3. Use Print receipt to print a paper copy or choose Save as PDF in the browser print dialog.

Marketplace fulfillment

Update the order as it moves through the marketplace workflow. Typical statuses are Pending, Packed, Shipped, Delivered, and Completed. Status changes do not create a new sale; they document fulfillment of the existing order.

Refund items — super administrator

1. Open the original transaction and select the item refund action.

2. Enter the quantity being refunded and a clear reason.

3. Choose whether returned goods should be restocked. Restock only products that are physically sellable and returned to inventory.

4. Confirm the refund. FlashPOS reduces net sales and returns the related portion of the percentage charge.

Refund control
Never delete or manually alter stock to imitate a refund. Use the refund feature so the sale, tax, stock movement, and activity log remain connected.

Receipt numbering

Use the FlashPOS receipt number when reconciling an order, responding to a buyer, or investigating a discrepancy. Marketplace IDs and GCash references support verification but do not replace the internal receipt number.

5. Inventory

Inventory tracks each product in pieces while allowing sales and stock adjustments in either pieces or boxes.

Add a product

1. Open Inventory and select Add product.

2. Enter product name, unique SKU, category, and optional barcode.

3. Enter cost per piece and piece selling price.

4. If sold by box, enter pieces per box and the box selling price. If it is not sold by box, set pieces per box to 1.

5. Enter opening boxes and loose pieces, then set the low-stock alert level.

6. Save and verify that the product appears with the correct on-hand quantity.

Adjust stock

MODEUSE WHENEXAMPLE
AddStock physically arrived outside a purchase receipt or a correction is needed+2 boxes after verified delivery
RemoveDamage, expiry, loss, or another non-sale reduction occurred−5 pieces damaged
Set exactA complete physical count has established the true quantitySet to 8 boxes and 3 pieces

Select Pieces or Boxes, enter the quantity, and provide a meaningful reason. The adjustment creates a stock movement and an activity record.

Edit, archive, or delete

ACTIONRESULTBEST USE
EditChanges product details and selling unitsCorrect names, pricing, barcode, or thresholds.
ArchiveHides a product from normal active use while preserving historyDiscontinued products with past transactions.
DeletePermanently removes an eligible productIncorrect test products with no required history; super admin only.
Count before correcting
Use Set exact only after a physical count. Record the reason in plain language, such as “Physical count 21 Jul 2026,” so the change can be audited.

6. Purchases and receiving

Purchasing records expected inventory and increases stock only when quantities are actually received.

Super administrator setup

1. Create the supplier with name and optional contact, phone, email, and address.

2. Create a purchase order, select the supplier, and add product lines in pieces with their cost per piece.

3. Add a supplier reference and notes when available, then save the purchase order.

Receive stock

1. Open Purchases and select the relevant purchase order.

2. Compare the delivery against each purchase-order line.

3. Enter only the quantity physically received. Partial receipts are allowed.

4. Confirm receipt. FlashPOS increases inventory and records the receiving activity.

5. Repeat later for any remaining quantity until the order is fully received.

Avoid duplicate stock
Do not also use an Add stock adjustment for items received through a purchase order. Receiving already increases inventory.

Receiving controls

CHECKWHY IT MATTERS
Correct supplier and POKeeps purchase history and vendor totals accurate.
Correct product and quantityPrevents overstatement or understatement of stock.
Correct cost per pieceSupports more reliable cost and income reporting.
Delivery conditionDamaged items should not be received as sellable stock.

Print a report

4. In the browser dialog, select a printer or Save as PDF. Review paper size, orientation, margins, and preview before printing.

Export data

CSV privacy
Exports may contain staff names, transaction references, costs, and operational history. Store them in a restricted folder and avoid sharing them publicly.

8. Daily closing

Daily closing compares what FlashPOS expects with what the cashier actually counted. It identifies shortages, overages, or missing records.

What the figures mean

FIELDMEANING
Expected cashToday’s recorded walk-in cash payments for the signed-in cashier.
Expected GCashToday’s recorded and verified walk-in GCash payments for the signed-in cashier.
Actual cashCash physically counted in the drawer for the applicable sales.
Actual GCashTotal verified in the GCash merchant/account history.
VarianceActual amount minus expected amount. Zero means the records match.

Close the cashier day

1. Stop processing sales for the cashier being closed.

2. Count the applicable cash carefully and verify the day’s GCash references.

3. Open Daily closing and compare Expected cash and Expected GCash.

4. Enter Actual cash and Actual GCash.

5. If a variance exists, investigate receipts, duplicate/missing sales, change, and GCash references.

6. Enter closing notes explaining any unresolved shortage, overage, or verification detail.

7. Select Close cashier day. Each cashier can close the day once; the result appears in Closing history.

Example variance
Expected cash ₱5,000; actual cash ₱4,950; variance −₱50. The note should explain the investigation or unresolved shortage.
Scope
Daily closing currently reconciles walk-in Cash and GCash. TikTok, Lazada, and Shopee settlements should be checked against their marketplace statements separately.

9. Expenses, income, and percentage tax

The Expenses workspace records actual operating costs by date and supports monthly income review for super administrators.

Record an expense

1. Open Expenses and select the month you want to review.

2. Select Add expense, enter the actual date, category, amount, and a useful note.

3. Save the entry and confirm it appears in Expense entries and Category totals.

4. Use Edit to correct an entry. Delete only duplicate or invalid records.

3% non-VAT percentage charge

FlashPOS automatically adds the configured 3% charge to every new transaction and displays it separately from sales. The Expenses page shows the amount collected for the selected month. When the business actually pays the tax, record that payment under Percentage-tax payment; do not record the collected estimate as an expense before payment unless that matches your accounting policy.

MONTHLY VIEWBASIC INTERPRETATION

Accounting notice
FlashPOS is an operational record, not a tax filing service. Confirm the correct tax base, classification, deductions, and filing treatment with your accountant or the appropriate tax authority.

10. Administration and system tools

Super administrators maintain access, review accountability, and use store-wide print and export tools.

Team and access

1. Open Team & access and create a user with name, email, temporary password, and role.

2. Assign Employee for normal operations or Super Admin only when full system control is required.

3. Tell the user to sign in and replace any shared temporary credentials according to store policy.

4. Deactivate users who should no longer access the store. Reactivate only after authorization.

Activity log

Activity log shows the latest recorded system actions, including the user, action type, affected record, details, and time. Use it to investigate stock changes, product edits, refunds, expenses, and administrative actions. The log is an audit record; do not treat it as a place to edit transactions.

11. Standard operating routines

Consistent routines make the reports trustworthy and reduce stock and payment discrepancies.

Opening checklist

• Sign in with your own account and confirm the correct user name and role.

• Check that System online appears and open the Dashboard.

• Review Stock attention and urgently needed purchase receipts.

• Confirm that the cash drawer and GCash device/account are ready.

• Review pending marketplace orders before accepting new fulfillment work.

During the day

• Record every order once, using the correct source and reference.

• Print or save receipts when required and keep the receipt number for inquiries.

• Receive purchase stock only after physically checking it.

• Record damage, expiry, and corrections as stock adjustments with clear reasons.

• Record actual expenses on their real dates and retain supporting documents.

• Update marketplace fulfillment statuses as orders progress.

End-of-day checklist

• Review Recent sales and compare marketplace orders with TikTok, Lazada, and Shopee.

• Verify GCash references and count the applicable cash.

• Complete Daily closing and explain any variance.

• Review low stock and prepare purchase orders or replenishment notes.

• Sign out of FlashPOS and secure printed reports and exported files.

One source of truth
Do not maintain a second unofficial sales total that bypasses FlashPOS. If a correction is needed, use the corresponding transaction, refund, expense, purchase, or stock function.

12. Troubleshooting and support

Use these checks before changing data. If the issue remains, capture the exact error message, time, page, and receipt or product reference.

ISSUECHECKS AND ACTION
Cannot sign inConfirm email/password, internet access, and that the account is active. Ask a super admin to verify the user.
Product does not appear in POSConfirm it is active, has a selling unit and price, and is not archived. Check stock if out-of-stock items are restricted.
Not enough stockVerify the requested piece/box quantity, review stock movements, and perform a physical count before adjusting.
Duplicate GCash or marketplace IDSearch Transactions for the reference. Confirm whether the order was already recorded.
Stock total is wrongReview sales, refunds, receipts, and stock movements; then use Set exact only after a physical count.
Report shows zero or wrong monthConfirm the selected period and transaction dates. Check whether refunds changed net sales.
Cash/GCash varianceCompare receipts, payment methods, change, GCash references, and missing or duplicate transactions.
Print layout is incorrectUse print preview, correct paper size/orientation, set reasonable margins, and disable unwanted browser headers.
Application unavailableRefresh once, check internet access, then ask the administrator to review Railway deployment/runtime logs.
Feature says migration requiredA super administrator must run the specified SQL migration in the connected Supabase project, then refresh.

Information to provide when asking for help

• Your name and role, without sharing your password.

• The page and action being performed.

• Exact error message and a screenshot with secret keys hidden.

• Receipt number, product SKU, purchase order, or date range involved.

• Whether the issue occurs locally, on the Railway production site, or both.

Protect secrets
Never include Supabase secret keys, passwords, or private environment-variable values in a screenshot or support message.
